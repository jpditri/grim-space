# Penance Sandwich

## Description
A Penance Sandwich carries the weight of its name in every bite, served in certain supernatural establishments around Boston as both sustenance and psychological experience.

## Physical Appearance
- **Bread**: Firm, austere crust of dark rye with sour tang
- **Texture**: Crackling crust that sets a sharp, confessional tone
- **Size**: Standard sandwich portions, typically served wrapped in brown paper

## Ingredients

### The Foundation
- **Dark Rye Bread**: Sourdough base with almost penitential sourness
- **Coarse Mustard**: Steeped with bitter herbs (wormwood, rue)
- **Tongue-Puckering Effect**: Leaves lasting taste of guilt and remorse

### The Filling
- **Salted Beef**: Thin ribbons pressed overnight in juniper-and-vinegar brine
- **Smoky Character**: Meat cured through hardship, penitential in nature
- **Pickled Roots**: Beet and parsnip slices offering sweet forgiveness
- **Curd Cheese**: Dense, slightly chalky texture providing cool contrast

### The Accents
- **Cracked Black Pepper**: Surprising warmth amid the austerity
- **Powdered Cinnamon**: Swirls through layers with unexpected comfort
- **Bitter Greens**: Dandelion or mustard leaves for sharp, cleansing bite

## Sensory Experience

### Taste Profile
- **First Impression**: Sharp, sour tang like confession half-spoken
- **Development**: Bitter herbs balanced by earthy sweetness
- **Aftertaste**: Lingering sourness mixed with distant sweetness, like a vow to do better

### Psychological Effect
- **Emotional Impact**: Each bite serves as both reproach and promise of forgiveness
- **Reflection**: Designed to remind consumer of failures and hope of absolution
- **Cathartic**: Eating becomes act of acknowledging guilt and seeking redemption

## Supernatural Properties

### Emotional Resonance
- **Guilt Amplification**: Supernatural beings with guilty consciences find the sandwich almost unbearable
- **Cleansing Effect**: Those who finish the entire sandwich may find temporary relief from supernatural curses related to guilt
- **Truth Compulsion**: Some claim eating the sandwich makes it difficult to lie for several hours

### Cultural Significance
- **Supernatural Justice**: Sometimes served as punishment in supernatural courts
- **Ritual Food**: Used in certain redemption ceremonies
- **Sacred Offering**: Given to supernatural beings seeking forgiveness
- **Test of Character**: Ability to finish the sandwich seen as measure of moral strength

## Where to Find
- **The Brass Tide Inn** (similar establishments in supernatural community)
- **Sacred Bakery** (specialty orders for ritual purposes)
- **Underground supernatural diners** throughout Boston area
- **Punishment establishments** in supernatural justice system

## Game Mechanics
- **Fortitude Save**: DC 12 Constitution save to finish entire sandwich without becoming nauseated
- **Supernatural Effect**: Advantage on saving throws against guilt-based supernatural attacks for 24 hours after consumption
- **Truth Compulsion**: Disadvantage on Deception checks for 4 hours after eating
- **Cleansing**: May remove one level of supernatural guilt-based curse or affliction

## Adventure Hooks
- **Ritual Requirement**: Character must consume penance sandwich as part of supernatural trial
- **Investigation Tool**: Sandwich affects supernatural criminals differently than innocents
- **Cultural Exchange**: Learning about supernatural food customs reveals community secrets
- **Punishment Assignment**: Character sentenced to daily penance sandwich consumption

## Variants
- **The Full Confession**: Double-sized version for serious transgressions
- **The Whispered Guilt**: Smaller version for minor supernatural infractions
- **The Absolution**: Special preparation that provides stronger cleansing effects
- **The Boston Special**: Local variant with added cranberries (slightly less bitter)
