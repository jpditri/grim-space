# Lynn Woods Reservation

- ## Overview
Lynn Woods Reservation is a 2,200-acre forest preserve in [[Lynn]], [[Massachusetts]], just north of [[Boston]]. While appearing as a typical New England woodland to mundane visitors, it serves as a supernatural sanctuary and neutral ground for the region's hidden magical community.

- ## Physical Description
- **Size**: 2,200 acres of mixed hardwood and pine forest
- **Terrain**: Rolling hills, walking trails, ponds, and rocky outcroppings
- **Notable Features**: Stone Tower (historic fire tower), several small ponds, extensive trail network
- **Public Access**: Open daily, popular with hikers, dog walkers, and joggers

- ## Supernatural Significance

	- ### Neutral Territory
- **Supernatural Sanctuary**: Acknowledged neutral ground between supernatural factions
- **Ancient Pacts**: Protected by treaties dating back centuries
- **Enforcement**: Violations of neutrality face severe supernatural justice
- **Safe Haven**: Supernatural beings can reveal their true forms within the deep woods

	- ### Magical Properties
- **Ley Line Convergence**: Multiple magical energy lines intersect beneath the forest
- **Veil Thinning**: Barrier between mortal and supernatural worlds is weaker here
- **Ritual Sites**: Hidden groves and stone circles for supernatural ceremonies
- **Time Distortion**: Some areas experience temporal anomalies

	- ### Hidden Locations
- **The Gathering Grove**: Secret meeting place for supernatural parliament
- **Whispering Pond**: Water reflects supernatural truth, shows hidden forms
- **The Stone Circle**: Ancient ritual site predating colonial settlement
- **The Old Oak**: Massive tree serves as message board for supernatural community

- ## Supernatural Inhabitants

	- ### The Forest Court
- **Woodland Spirits**: Dryads, nature spirits, and tree shepherds
- **The Green Man**: Ancient protector of the reservation
- **Fae Creatures**: Seasonal courts maintain presence in deeper woods
- **Guardian Beasts**: Supernatural creatures protect sacred sites

	- ### Regular Visitors
- **Supernatural Refugees**: Beings seeking sanctuary from city dangers
- **Ritual Practitioners**: Witches, shamans, and other magic users
- **Information Brokers**: Neutral ground perfect for exchanging secrets
- **Young Supernaturals**: Safe place to learn to control abilities

- ## Mundane Cover
- **Conservation Status**: Protected as state park, limits development
- **Regular Visitors**: Mundane hikers provide excellent cover for supernatural activity
- **Ranger Presence**: Park rangers unknowingly help maintain secrecy
- **Educational Programs**: Nature walks and school groups mask supernatural gatherings

- ## Adventure Hooks
- **Treaty Negotiations**: Characters mediate supernatural territorial disputes
- **Ritual Interruption**: Mundane hikers stumble into supernatural ceremony
- **Ancient Secrets**: Archaeological survey discovers supernatural artifacts
- **Sanctuary Violation**: Someone breaks the neutral ground rules
- **Lost Mundanes**: Hikers enter areas with time distortion effects

- ## Dangers
- **Territorial Disputes**: Even neutral ground has boundaries
- **Mundane Exposure**: Risk of revealing supernatural world to hikers
- **Ancient Guardians**: Some protections are aggressive toward intruders
- **Temporal Anomalies**: Easy to become lost in time-shifted areas
- **Seasonal Courts**: Fae politics can be deadly for mortals

- ## Game Master Notes
- Perfect location for supernatural meetings and negotiations
- Natural barrier between mundane [[Boston]] and supernatural community
- Multiple entry points allow for various encounter types
- Weather and seasons affect supernatural activity levels
- Rangers can serve as unwitting allies or complications
