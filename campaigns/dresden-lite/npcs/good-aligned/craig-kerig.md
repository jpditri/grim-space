- # Craig Kerig
- ## The Night Watchman with Stories

- ### Physical Description
- Craig is a round man in his 40s with the comfortable bulk of someone who enjoys good food and coffee—lots of coffee. His security uniform strains slightly across his middle, and he has the relaxed posture of someone who's comfortable in his own skin. Friendly brown eyes peer from behind a face that's quick to smile, and his receding hairline is offset by a well-maintained mustache. He almost always has a steaming cup of coffee in hand.

- **Most Notable Feature**: Never seen without his coffee mug—a large, worn ceramic mug with "World's Okayest Dad" printed on the side, a gift from his children.

- ### Personality Traits
- **Storyteller**: Loves sharing tales about the museum's supernatural activity
- **Family Man**: Devoted to wife Julia and his two children
- **Coffee Addict**: Drinks coffee throughout his entire shift
- **Veteran Perspective**: His Coast Guard experience gives him a practical, no-nonsense approach to unusual situations
- **Loyal**: Extremely protective of the museum and its contents

- ### Background
		- Before taking his current position at the Peabody Essex Museum, Craig served in the U.S. Coast Guard, stationed on Winter Island in [[Salem]]. This posting gave him firsthand experience with the island's strange phenomena, though he didn't fully understand the supernatural implications at the time. After his military service, he settled into civilian life as a security guard, appreciating the steady work and time with his family.

- ### Current Situation
- **Night Shift**: Works the graveyard shift at the museum, 11 PM to 7 AM
- **Supernatural Witness**: Has encountered genuine supernatural phenomena in the museum
- **Winter Island Knowledge**: Possesses valuable information about strange activities on Winter Island
- **Family Concerns**: Growing worried about the recent disappearances, especially as a parent

- ### Key Information He Possesses
- **Winter Island Activities**: Witnessed strange lights, sounds, and "training exercises" during his Coast Guard service
- **Museum Hauntings**: Knows which exhibits are genuinely haunted vs. tourist trap stories
- **Timeline Knowledge**: Can correlate recent strange activities with the disappearances
- **Security Protocols**: Understands how to access restricted areas and archives

- ### Winter Island Experience
		- During his Coast Guard service, Craig witnessed:
- Strange lights appearing over the abandoned estate
- Unexplained radio interference during certain weather conditions
- Wildlife avoiding specific areas of the island
- Fellow servicemen reporting "training exercises" that didn't match official schedules
- Boats approaching the island at odd hours with no logged permission

- ### Museum Ghost Stories
		- **True Stories** (Actual Supernatural Activity):
- Civil War soldier's spirit in the maritime exhibit
- Native American artifacts that move on their own
- Cold spots that follow specific visitor patterns
- Security cameras malfunctioning in the Asian art wing

- **Embellished Stories** (Enhanced for Entertainment):
- The "Cursed Portrait" that watches visitors
- Phantom footsteps in the colonial house exhibit
- Books that open themselves in the library section

- ### Personal Life
- **Wife**: Julia Kerig (works at [[Salem]] Hospital as a nurse)
- **Children**: Two kids, ages 12 and 15
- **Dog**: Brody, a golden retriever who's getting old but still loves walks
- **Hobbies**: Coaching little league, fishing, collecting vintage coffee mugs

- ### Roleplaying Notes
		- **Voice**: Warm, slightly gravelly from years of coffee and late nights
		- **Mannerisms**:
- Takes a sip of coffee before answering important questions
- Leans forward conspiratorially when sharing "real" ghost stories
- Pulls out wallet photos of his family when conversation turns personal
- Taps his security badge when emphasizing his authority/access

- **Conversation Starters**:
- "You know, most folks think these ghost stories are all made up for the tourists..."
- *[Sips coffee]* "Back when I was stationed on Winter Island, we used to see the strangest things..."
- "Julia—that's my wife—she thinks I'm crazy, but I know what I've seen in this place."

- ### Potential Plot Hooks
- **Winter Island Guide**: Can provide detailed layout and access information
- **Museum Access**: Able to provide after-hours entry to restricted collections
- **Family Connection**: His wife Julia at [[Salem]] Hospital might know about [[Madison Scott]]
- **Supernatural Validation**: His stories help confirm supernatural activity patterns

- ### Character Arc Opportunities
- Protecting his family from the escalating supernatural threats
- Using his Coast Guard training to help in dangerous situations
- Reconciling his practical nature with supernatural reality
- Becoming a crucial ally for nighttime investigations

		- **AC**: 12 (Security Uniform + Dex)
		- **HP**: 38 (7d8+7)
		- **Speed**: 30 ft

- **STR**: 13 (+1) **DEX**: 14 (+2) **CON**: 13 (+1)
- **INT**: 12 (+1) **WIS**: 15 (+2) **CHA**: 14 (+2)

- **Skills**: Perception +5, Investigation +4, Athletics +4, Insight +5
- **Languages**: English, basic Spanish
- **Proficiencies**: Security systems, basic first aid, boats/watercraft

- **Special Abilities**:
- **Museum Knowledge**: Familiar with all museum layouts, security, and collections
- **Winter Island Experience**: Detailed knowledge of island geography and access points
- **Night Vision**: Years of night shift work have enhanced his low-light vision
- **Radio Network**: Maintains contact with other security personnel across [[Salem]]

- **Equipment**: Security radio, flashlight, coffee thermos, museum keys, cell phone
