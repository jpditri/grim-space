# Paul Rhodes
## The Dispensary Owner with Secrets

### Physical Description
Paul is a man in his mid-50s with the comfortable, approachable appearance of someone who works in customer service. He's slightly overweight with a soft build that suggests he enjoys good food and beer. His most striking feature is a large, well-maintained red beard that reaches nearly to his chest—he takes obvious pride in its fullness and color. His head is balding on top, creating a dramatic contrast with his impressive facial hair. He typically wears flannel shirts and jeans, with the casual demeanor of someone who's found his niche in life.

**Most Notable Feature**: His magnificent red beard, which he often strokes thoughtfully during conversations, especially when discussing business matters.

### Personality Traits
- **Easy to Talk To**: Natural conversationalist who puts customers at ease
- **Dual Nature**: Maintains completely separate personas for his two businesses
- **Protective**: Takes gun safety and customer screening very seriously
- **Independent**: Bachelor by choice, enjoys his freedom and flexibility
- **Practical**: Approaches problems with straightforward, no-nonsense solutions

### Background
Paul has operated the Purple Dragon dispensary on Essex Street for three years, building a reputation as a knowledgeable and friendly cannabis retailer. Unknown to his daytime customers, he also runs a carefully managed gun smuggling operation out of his family's defunct "Leather Works" business on the wharf. This operation serves people who need protection from supernatural threats but can't access weapons through legal channels.

### Current Situation
- **Legal Business**: Thriving dispensary with regular customers and steady income
- **Illegal Business**: Gun smuggling operation serving supernatural community
- **Increased Demand**: Recent disappearances have driven up demand for protection
- **Security Concerns**: Worried about increased law enforcement attention

### The Purple Dragon Dispensary
**Location**: Essex Street, Salem
**Cover**: Legitimate medical and recreational marijuana dispensary
**Customers**: Tourists, locals, some supernatural community members seeking medicinal products
**Specialties**: High-quality strains, CBD products, edibles
**Reputation**: Known for knowledgeable service and consistent product quality

### The Leather Works Operation
**Location**: The Wharf, Salem (family's old leather clothing business)
**Purpose**: Illegal firearms sales to supernatural community
**Products**: Handguns, shotguns, ammunition, basic tactical gear
**Clients**: Supernatural beings, humans aware of supernatural threats, monster hunters
**Security**: Carefully vetted customers, coded language, cash-only transactions

### Key Information He Possesses
- **Supernatural Customer Base**: Knows many members of the hidden community
- **Threat Assessment**: Understands which supernatural entities are dangerous
- **Recent Activity**: Has noticed increased weapon requests since disappearances began
- **Law Enforcement**: Knows which cops to avoid and which might be sympathetic

### Business Philosophy
Paul operates both businesses with strict ethical codes:
- **Dispensary**: Refuses sales to minors, provides accurate product information, maintains medical privacy
- **Gun Sales**: Only sells to those facing genuine supernatural threats, requires basic safety knowledge, refuses sales to anyone seeking to harm innocents

### Personal Life
- **Unmarried**: Never married, no children, values his independence
- **Social**: Enjoys conversations with customers, regulars consider him a friend
- **Hobbies**: Fishing, Red Sox games, maintaining his beard, woodworking
- **Living Situation**: Apartment above the dispensary for convenience and security

### Roleplaying Notes
**Voice**: Warm, slightly gravelly, with a Boston accent
**Mannerisms**:
- Strokes his beard when thinking or discussing business
- Leans on the dispensary counter in a relaxed, welcoming manner
- Uses different body language entirely at the gun operation (more alert, cautious)
- Always offers customers coffee or tea while they browse

**Conversation Starters**:
- "First time in? Let me tell you about our most popular strains..."
- *[Strokes beard thoughtfully]* "You look like someone who might appreciate quality products."
- "Business has been... interesting lately. People seem more on edge, you know?"

### Code Words and Signals
For his gun operation, Paul uses specific phrases:
- "Looking for leather goods" = Interested in firearms
- "Family business" = Confirms legitimate supernatural threat
- "Quality materials" = High-end weapons available
- "Custom work" = Special ammunition (silver, blessed, etc.)

### Potential Plot Hooks
- **Weapon Source**: Can provide characters with necessary firearms
- **Information Network**: Knows who's buying weapons and why
- **Safe Haven**: Dispensary serves as neutral ground for information exchange
- **Community Connections**: Bridge between mundane and supernatural customers

### Moral Complexity
Paul operates in legal and ethical gray areas:
- Believes people have the right to defend themselves against supernatural threats
- Refuses to sell to anyone he suspects might harm innocents
- Maintains strict customer confidentiality for both businesses
- Struggles with the inherent dangers of his gun operation

**AC**: 13 (Leather Jacket + Dex)
**HP**: 45 (10d8+10)
**Speed**: 30 ft

**STR**: 12 (+1) **DEX**: 16 (+3) **CON**: 13 (+1)
**INT**: 14 (+2) **WIS**: 15 (+2) **CHA**: 16 (+3)

**Languages**: English, basic Spanish
**Proficiencies**: Firearms, business management, basic mechanical repair

**Special Abilities**:
- **Street Network**: Extensive contacts in both legitimate and underground communities
- **Weapon Expertise**: Knowledgeable about firearms, ammunition, and tactical equipment
- **Customer Assessment**: Excellent judge of character and motivation
- **Dual Identity Management**: Expertly maintains separation between his two operations

**Equipment**: 
- Dispensary: Cash register, product inventory, security system
- Gun Operation: Various firearms, ammunition, basic tactical gear
