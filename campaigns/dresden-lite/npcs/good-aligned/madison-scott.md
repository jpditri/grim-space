- # [[Madison Scott]]
- ## The Broken Witness

- ### Physical Description
- Madison is a woman in her 20s who appears older than her years due to the trauma she's endured. She has unkempt blonde hair that hangs limp around her face, and pale blue eyes that seem to stare through people rather than at them. Her frame is thin from poor appetite and the stress of her mental condition. She typically wears hospital-issued clothing, and her movements alternate between periods of agitated pacing and complete stillness. When she's lucid, glimpses of her former self show through—intelligent, articulate, and deeply frightened.

- **Most Notable Feature**: Her eyes, which occasionally show moments of complete terror as if she's seeing something no one else can perceive.

- ### Personality Traits
- **Fractured Reality**: Struggles to distinguish between memories, hallucinations, and current reality
- **Intelligent (When Lucid)**: During clear moments, displays sharp intelligence and awareness
- **Traumatized**: Carries deep psychological wounds from supernatural encounter
- **Protective**: Despite her condition, tries to warn others about the dangers she's witnessed
- **Vulnerable**: Completely dependent on others for care and protection

- ### Background
		- Madison had a difficult childhood with abusive parents, leading her to leave home at 16 and survive on her own. She developed a drug addiction during her teenage years and has been in and out of rehabilitation clinics ever since. Recently, she had achieved sobriety and was trying to rebuild her life when she attended a party with the group that would later form the Coven of the Crimson Pages. During their early experiments with The Black Book, Madison was unfortunate enough to look directly into the eyes of an aberrant horror, which invaded her mind and showed her visions of the far realm it originated from. This encounter shattered her sanity.

- ### Current Situation
- **Hospitalized**: Confined to [[Salem]] Hospital's mental health ward
- **Misdiagnosed**: Doctors believe she's suffering from drug-induced psychosis
- **Crucial Witness**: Possesses firsthand knowledge of the Coven's early activities
- **Mental State**: Rapidly deteriorating due to ongoing psychic contamination
- **Time-Sensitive**: Her condition will worsen unless the supernatural influence is addressed

- ### The Supernatural Encounter
		- **The Party**: Madison attended a gathering where Henry Abbot first experimented with The Black Book
		- **The Summoning**: She witnessed the Coven's first successful entity summoning
		- **Eye Contact**: She looked directly into the aberrant horror's eyes
		- **Mental Invasion**: The entity forced visions of its home dimension into her mind
		- **Aftermath**: The Coven abandoned her when she began screaming and couldn't stop

- ### What She Witnessed
		- Madison's fractured memories contain crucial information:
- **The Black Book**: She saw Henry reading from the grimoire
- **Summoning Ritual**: Details about how the Coven conducts their ceremonies
- **Entity Description**: Accurate knowledge of the aberrant horror's appearance and abilities
- **Coven Members**: Recognition of several cultists, including Henry and early followers
- **Dimensional Visions**: Disturbing insights into the far realm entities

- ### Mental State Symptoms
		- **Psychotic Episodes**: Sudden outbursts describing otherworldly visions
		- **Catatonic Periods**: Hours of complete unresponsiveness
		- **Fragmented Speech**: Mixes real memories with dimensional visions
		- **Sleep Disorders**: Chronic nightmares and inability to achieve restful sleep
		- **Reality Distortion**: Cannot distinguish between hospital and the far realm

- ### Medical Treatment
		- **Current Diagnosis**: Drug-induced psychosis with possible schizophreniform disorder
		- **Medications**: Antipsychotics and anxiolytics that provide minimal relief
		- **Therapy**: Standard psychiatric counseling that doesn't address supernatural causes
		- **Prognosis**: Poor, with doctors expecting continued deterioration
		- **Family**: No supportive family members involved in her care

- ### Supernatural Contamination
		- Madison's mind has been infected by exposure to the far realm:
- **Ongoing Visions**: The entity continues to show her disturbing images
- **Psychic Echoes**: Her mind occasionally connects to the aberrant horror's consciousness
- **Deteriorating Barriers**: The boundary between her consciousness and the entity's is breaking down
- **Time Limit**: Without intervention, she will be permanently lost to madness

- ### Memory Recovery Attempts
		- [[Beverly Martinez]] could potentially help Madison recover coherent memories, but the risks are extreme:
- **Success**: Could provide detailed intelligence about Coven operations
- **Partial Success**: Might recover fragments while stabilizing her current condition
- **Failure**: Complete mental destruction and permanent catatonia
- **Dangerous Side Effects**: Could strengthen the entity's connection to her mind

- ### Key Information She Possesses
- **Coven Membership**: Can identify Henry Abbot and original cultists
- **Ritual Details**: Witnessed early summoning attempts and their methods
- **Entity Capabilities**: Understands the aberrant horror's powers and weaknesses
- **Dimensional Knowledge**: Involuntary access to far realm information
- **Timeline**: Can provide dates and locations of early Coven activities

- ### Hospital Environment
		- **Ward Security**: Moderate security, focused on patient safety rather than external threats
		- **Staff Access**: Nurses, doctors, social workers, and janitorial staff
		- **Visitor Restrictions**: Limited visiting hours, background checks for non-family
		- **Record Keeping**: Detailed medical records documenting her condition's progression
		- **Other Patients**: Several other individuals with various mental health conditions

- ### Roleplaying Notes
		- **Voice**: Varies between whispered rambling and sudden shouts, often switching mid-sentence
		- **Mannerisms**:
- Stares at empty spaces as if seeing things others cannot
- Draws abstract symbols on paper when given art supplies
- Flinches away from eye contact, especially with strangers
- Occasionally speaks coherently about mundane topics before spiraling back into visions

- **Lucid Moments**:
- "They opened something... something that should have stayed closed."
- "The book... Henry found a book, and it found him back."
- "You have to stop them before Halloween. They're going to bring it all the way through."

- **Psychotic Episodes**:
- "The eyes! Don't look at the eyes! It sees everything!"
- "The red sky is bleeding into our world!"
- "They're coming through the cracks, all of them, so many..."

- ### Potential Plot Hooks
- **Key Witness**: Essential for understanding early Coven activities
- **Rescue Mission**: Players might need to extract her from the hospital
- **Memory Recovery**: [[Beverly Martinez]] could attempt to heal her mind
- **Information Source**: Her visions might reveal current Coven plans
- **Supernatural Detector**: Her condition worsens when supernatural activity increases nearby

- ### Treatment Options
		- **Conventional Medicine**: Will continue to fail without addressing supernatural causes
		- **Memory Suppression**: Magic could hide traumatic memories but wouldn't cure the contamination
		- **Spiritual Cleansing**: Exorcism or purification rituals might sever the entity connection
		- **Professional Care**: Transfer to supernatural-aware medical facility
		- **Magical Healing**: High-level restoration magic could repair the psychic damage

- ### Character Arc Opportunities
- Recovering enough sanity to become an active ally
- Using her connection to the entity to gather intelligence
- Finding peace through helping stop the Coven that destroyed her mind
- Rebuilding her life after supernatural healing

		- **AC**: 10 (Hospital Gown)

- **STR**: 8 (-1) **DEX**: 10 (+0) **CON**: 10 (+0)
- **INT**: 14 (+2) **WIS**: 6 (-2) **CHA**: 8 (-1)

- **Skills**: None reliable due to mental state
- **Languages**: English (when coherent)
- **Condition**: Psychotic episodes, supernatural contamination

- **Special Abilities**:
- **Dimensional Sight**: Can occasionally perceive supernatural entities and activities
- **Prophetic Visions**: Visions sometimes reveal future Coven activities
- **Entity Connection**: Unwilling psychic link to aberrant horror
- **Supernatural Memory**: Retains perfect recall of supernatural encounters despite mental fragmentation

- **Vulnerabilities**:
- **Mental Fragility**: Automatically fails most Wisdom saves
- **Supernatural Sensitivity**: Takes psychic damage when exposed to aberrant magic
- **Dependency**: Requires constant medical supervision and care
