- # Twink
- ## The Winter Island Fairy

- ### Physical Description
		- Twink is a small female fairy, standing only about 6 inches tall with delicate, humanoid features. She has gossamer wings that shimmer with an opalescent quality, shifting between silver and pale blue depending on the light. Her hair is white-blonde and seems to move in its own breeze, and her eyes are large and luminous—a pale lavender color that seems almost too large for her face. She typically wears clothing woven from flower petals, leaves, and spider silk that changes with the seasons. Despite her tiny size, she radiates an ancient intelligence and otherworldly presence.

- **Most Notable Feature**: Her wings create a very faint, musical humming sound when she flies, like tiny wind chimes in a gentle breeze.

- ### Personality Traits
- **Ancient Wisdom**: Despite her small size, she possesses knowledge spanning centuries
- **Cautious**: Extremely careful about revealing herself to humans
- **Protective**: Fiercely guards Winter Island's natural supernatural balance
- **Observant**: Watches everything that happens on the island without interfering
- **Cryptic**: Speaks in riddles and metaphors, typical of fairy communication

- ### Background
		- Twink has lived on Winter Island for over two centuries, serving as a guardian of the island's natural magical energies. She was present when the Puritans first established settlements and has witnessed the ebb and flow of supernatural activity in the area. The island's abandoned estate was once a place of neutral magical energy, but the Coven's activities have begun to corrupt the natural balance she protects.

- ### Current Situation
- **Territory Violation**: The Coven has claimed her island for their dark rituals
- **Magical Corruption**: Their activities are poisoning the natural supernatural energies
- **Witness Protection**: She observes Coven activities but cannot directly intervene
- **Seeking Help**: Increasingly desperate to find someone who can stop the corruption

- ### Fairy Magic and Limitations
		- **Abilities**: Flight, invisibility to most humans, minor nature magic, enhanced senses
		- **Limitations**: Cannot directly harm humans, bound to the island, vulnerable to iron
		- **Detection**: Can only be seen by those with supernatural awareness or fairy magic knowledge
		- **Communication**: Speaks an archaic form of English mixed with Gaelic phrases

- ### Knowledge of the Coven
		- Twink has been watching the Coven's activities for months:
- **Ritual Locations**: Knows exactly where ceremonies are conducted
- **Timing Patterns**: Understands their lunar and celestial timing
- **Participants**: Can identify Henry and other regular attendees
- **Magical Corruption**: Senses how their rituals damage the island's natural energies
- **Escalation**: Aware that their activities are building toward something catastrophic

- ### What She's Witnessed
		- **Monthly Rituals**: Observes every Coven gathering from hidden vantage points
		- **Entity Summoning**: Has seen the aberrant horrors they bring through dimensional barriers
		- **Abigail's Transformation**: Witnesses Henry's sister being prepared as a vessel
		- **Energy Corruption**: Feels the island's natural magic being twisted for dark purposes
		- **Future Plans**: Overhears discussions about the Halloween summoning

- ### The Name "Henry"
		- Twink knows one name from the Coven because:
- Henry often speaks loudly during rituals, issuing commands
- Other cultists address him directly by name
- She's overheard private conversations between Henry and Abigail
- His leadership role makes him the most visible member to outside observers

- ### Fairy Magic Knowledge
		- **Old Ways**: Twink understands ancient fairy magic that predates modern supernatural practices
		- **Ward Creation**: Can teach protective magic using natural materials
		- **Cleansing Rituals**: Knows how to purify corrupted magical areas
		- **Communication**: Can translate between supernatural languages and human speech
		- **Detection**: Her senses can identify supernatural threats and magical contamination

- ### Island Geography Knowledge
		- **Secret Paths**: Knows hidden routes around the island
		- **Abandoned Estate**: Familiar with every room and passage in the old building
		- **Natural Barriers**: Understands which areas provide protection or concealment
		- **Magical Nodes**: Aware of the island's natural power sources and how they're being corrupted
		- **Escape Routes**: Knows the safest ways to approach or leave the island undetected

- ### Roleplaying Notes
		- **Voice**: High-pitched, musical, with an archaic speaking pattern
		- **Mannerisms**:
- Hovers at eye level when speaking to humans
- Wings flutter rapidly when excited or nervous
- Speaks in metaphors related to nature and seasons
- Becomes very still when listening for danger

- **Speech Patterns**:
- "The old magics weep for what these mortals do to sacred ground."
- "Henry-who-reads-from-shadows brings poison to the island's heart."
- "When moon-sister is full again, they will tear the veils asunder."

- ### Finding Twink
		- Twink will only reveal herself to:
- Characters with knowledge of old fairy magic
- Those who show proper respect for natural supernatural forces
- People who approach Winter Island with pure intentions
- Anyone carrying protective charms made from natural materials

- **Requirements for Contact**:
- Must be alone or in very small groups
- Approach during dawn or dusk when the veils are thin
- Bring offerings of honey, milk, or fresh flowers
- Speak respectfully to the island itself before seeking her

- ### Potential Plot Hooks
- **Intelligence Source**: Can provide detailed information about Coven activities
- **Island Guide**: Essential for safely navigating Winter Island
- **Magical Assistance**: Can teach protective spells and ward creation
- **Timing Information**: Knows exactly when the next rituals will occur
- **Ancient Knowledge**: Understands the historical context of the Coven's magic

- ### Character Arc Opportunities
- Seeking champions to restore the island's natural balance
- Teaching ancient magic to worthy students
- Coordinating with other supernatural entities against the Coven
- Protecting the island's magical ecosystem from further corruption

- ### Fairy Politics and Obligations
		- **Territorial Duty**: Bound to protect Winter Island's supernatural balance
		- **Non-Interference Rules**: Cannot directly attack humans, even evil ones
		- **Ancient Pacts**: Operates under old magical laws that limit her actions
		- **Community Responsibility**: Represents other hidden fae in the area
		- **Long-term Perspective**: Thinks in decades and centuries, not days and weeks

- ### Stats & Abilities
		- **AC**: 15 (Natural Agility + Size)
		- **HP**: 7 (2d4+2)
		- **Speed**: 10 ft, fly 60 ft

- **STR**: 2 (-4) **DEX**: 20 (+5) **CON**: 14 (+2)
- **INT**: 16 (+3) **WIS**: 18 (+4) **CHA**: 16 (+3)

- **Skills**: Perception +7, Stealth +8, Nature +6, Arcana +6
- **Languages**: English (archaic), Gaelic, Sylvan, Celestial
- **Damage Vulnerabilities**: Cold iron weapons

- **Special Abilities**:
- **Invisibility**: Can become invisible at will, lasting until she attacks or loses concentration
- **Fairy Magic**: Can cast minor nature-based spells (druidcraft, speak with animals, detect magic)
- **Supernatural Senses**: Can perceive all supernatural entities and magical auras within 120 feet
- **Flight**: Natural flying speed with perfect maneuverability
- **Size Advantage**: Advantage on Stealth checks due to tiny size

- **Spell-like Abilities** (3/day each):
- *Faerie Fire*, *Entangle*, *Speak with Animals*

- **Limitations**:
- Cannot leave Winter Island for more than 24 hours without weakening
- Takes double damage from iron weapons
- Must retreat if directly threatened with violence
