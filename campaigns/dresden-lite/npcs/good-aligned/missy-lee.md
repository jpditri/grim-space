- # Missy Lee
- ## The Traumatized Witness

- ### Physical Description
		- Missy is a woman in her late 20s with the lean build of someone who stays active and works on her feet. She has straight black hair that she often wears in a practical bun while working, and dark eyes that carry a haunted quality she tries to hide behind professional smiles. Her arms and hands show several tattoos—both her own artistic work and pieces done by others in the shop. She moves with the efficient grace of someone comfortable in service work, but occasionally displays moments of sudden alertness, as if listening for danger.

- **Most Notable Feature**: A small scar near her left temple that she covers with makeup and hair styling—a reminder of her trauma that she doesn't want to explain.

- ### Personality Traits
- **Hardworking**: Maintains two jobs with professional competence
- **Artistic**: Genuinely talented tattoo artist with growing reputation
- **Survivor**: Tough and resilient, but carrying hidden psychological wounds
- **Protective**: Instinctively shields others from potential harm
- **Memory Gaps**: Struggles with missing time and fragmented recollections

- ### Background
		- Missy works at Hong Kong Kings Chinese restaurant just off Essex Street and also has a side job at a local tattoo parlor. Several weeks ago, she was the sole surviving witness to a horrific attack in Forest River Park where a group of teenagers were violently assaulted by an aberrant horror summoned by the Coven. The traumatic encounter left her with suppressed memories and PTSD, though she can't consciously remember the supernatural aspects of what happened.

- ### Current Situation
- **Dual Employment**: Balances restaurant work with growing tattoo business
- **Trauma Symptoms**: Experiencing nightmares, anxiety attacks, and memory gaps
- **Unconscious Knowledge**: Holds crucial information about the Coven's activities
- **Vulnerable State**: Could be targeted by the Coven to prevent her from remembering

- ### The Forest River Park Incident
		- **What Happened**: Missy was with a group of teenagers who were attacked by an aberrant horror
		- **The Attack**: The entity brutally killed the other teens while Missy hid
		- **Her Survival**: She escaped but not before witnessing the horror's true form
		- **Memory Suppression**: Her mind blocked the supernatural aspects to preserve her sanity
		- **Physical Evidence**: The scar on her temple from debris during her escape

- ### Suppressed Memories
		- Missy's subconscious retains crucial information:
- The appearance and behavior of the aberrant horror
- Details about the teenagers who were targeted
- Sounds and smells associated with the attack (sulfur, otherworldly vocalizations)
- Glimpses of figures in robes who may have been controlling the entity
- The specific location where the attack occurred

- ### Work Life
		- **Hong Kong Kings Restaurant**:
- **Position**: Waitress and prep cook
- **Schedule**: Evening shifts, 4 PM to closing
- **Coworkers**: Generally well-liked, but they've noticed her recent changes
- **Customers**: Serves a mix of locals and tourists

- **Tattoo Parlor**:
- **Position**: Tattoo artist (part-time)
- **Schedule**: Weekends and some weekday afternoons
- **Specialties**: Fine line work, traditional Asian designs, cover-up work
- **Reputation**: Building a clientele through word-of-mouth and social media

- ### Trauma Symptoms
		- **Nightmares**: Recurring dreams of shadows and screaming
		- **Hypervigilance**: Constantly scanning for threats, especially in Forest River Park area
		- **Memory Gaps**: Can't remember the hour before or after the attack
		- **Physical Reactions**: Panic attacks triggered by sulfur smells or certain sounds
		- **Social Withdrawal**: Less outgoing than before, avoids discussing the incident

- ### Unlocking Her Memories
		- [[Beverly Martinez]] could potentially help Missy recover her memories through psychic channeling, but the process is dangerous:
- **Success**: Could provide detailed information about the Coven's methods
- **Failure**: Risk of complete mental breakdown or memory wipe
- **Partial Success**: Might recover fragments while leaving her vulnerable to further trauma

- ### Key Information She Possesses
- **Horror Description**: Witnessed the aberrant entity's true form and capabilities
- **Attack Pattern**: Understands how the Coven targets victims
- **Geographic Details**: Knows the exact location and timing of the attack
- **Coven Presence**: May have glimpsed the cultists directing the entity

- ### Roleplaying Notes
		- **Voice**: Quiet, measured, occasionally trails off when approaching traumatic memories
		- **Mannerisms**:
- Unconsciously touches the scar on her temple when stressed
- Exceptional spatial awareness, always knows where exits are located
- Hands shake slightly when discussing Forest River Park
- Displays her tattoo work with pride—it's her form of self-expression and control

- **Conversation Starters**:
- "Sorry, I don't really like talking about what happened in the park..."
- "I've been having the strangest dreams lately. Probably just stress from work."
- "There's something about that place... I can't explain it, but I won't go back there."

- ### Potential Plot Hooks
- **Key Witness**: Her recovered memories could expose the Coven's methods
- **Victim Protection**: The Coven might target her to prevent her from remembering
- **Survivor's Guilt**: Helping her process trauma could unlock cooperation
- **Local Connections**: Through her jobs, she knows many people in the community

- ### Character Arc Opportunities
- Recovering her memories and confronting the truth about the attack
- Using her artistic skills to help document supernatural threats
- Overcoming trauma to become an active ally in stopping the Coven
- Connecting with other survivors of supernatural encounters

- ### Artistic Abilities
		- Missy's tattoo work could be valuable for supernatural purposes:
- **Protective Symbols**: Could learn to ink supernatural protection ward tattoos
- **Documentation**: Her artistic eye could help create accurate depictions of entities
- **Community Connection**: The tattoo parlor serves as a gathering place for various subcultures
- **Income Source**: Her growing reputation could provide financial independence

- ### Stats & Abilities
		- **AC**: 13 (Dex + Intuition)
		- **HP**: 27 (6d8+6)
		- **Speed**: 30 ft

- **STR**: 10 (+0) **DEX**: 16 (+3) **CON**: 12 (+1)
- **INT**: 14 (+2) **WIS**: 13 (+1) **CHA**: 15 (+2)

- **Skills**: Perception +4, Stealth +6, Sleight of Hand +6, Deception +5
- **Languages**: English, Mandarin, Cantonese
- **Proficiencies**: Tattoo artistry, restaurant service, basic self-defense

- **Special Abilities**:
- **Suppressed Memories**: Contains crucial information about Coven activities
- **Survivor's Instincts**: Advantage on Perception checks to detect ambushes
- **Artistic Documentation**: Can create detailed visual records of supernatural encounters
- **Trauma Response**: Panic attacks in certain situations, but also enhanced danger awareness

- **Equipment**: Tattoo equipment, work uniforms, smartphone, pepper spray, car keys

- ### Recovery Risks
		- Attempting to recover Missy's memories involves significant dangers:
- **Mental Breakdown**: Complete psychological collapse if the process fails
- **Memory Wipe**: Risk of losing all memories, not just the traumatic ones
- **Coven Awareness**: The process might alert the Coven to her recovered knowledge
- **Supernatural Backlash**: The entity might have left psychic traces that could activate
