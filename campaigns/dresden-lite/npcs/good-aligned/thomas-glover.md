# Thomas Glover
## The Grieving Bookseller

### Physical Description
Thomas is a man in his 60s with the weathered appearance of someone who has spent decades surrounded by dusty tomes and old knowledge. His frame appears frail and slightly hunched, suggesting years of poor health or recent decline. Deep-set eyes behind wire-rimmed glasses reflect both intelligence and profound sadness. His gray hair is unkempt, and his clothes—typically wool sweaters and worn slacks—hang loosely on his diminished frame.

**Most Notable Feature**: A persistent, deep chest cough that sounds like it originates from the very depths of his lungs, often punctuating his conversations and causing him to pause mid-sentence.

### Personality Traits
- **Default Demeanor**: Grumpy and short-tempered with customers
- **Emotional Trigger**: Becomes weepy and soft when his deceased wife is mentioned
- **Knowledge Repository**: Possesses extensive occult knowledge through decades of book dealing
- **Protective Instinct**: Despite his gruff exterior, he genuinely cares about protecting people from dangerous supernatural knowledge

### Background
Thomas has operated the **Caldron of Books** on Essex Street for over thirty years, specializing in rare and occult texts. His wife Margaret passed away the previous year, leaving him devastated and increasingly isolated. He's one of the few mundane humans who understands the supernatural world exists, having learned through years of dealing with customers seeking genuine magical knowledge.

### Current Situation
- **Recent Loss**: Still grieving his wife's death, which has made him more irritable and suspicious
- **Dangerous Inventory**: Possesses several books that could be connected to the Coven's activities
- **Potential Witness**: May have sold materials to Henry Abbot or other Coven members
- **Knowledge Source**: Understands the significance of missing occult texts and ritual components

### Key Information He Possesses
- **The Black Book's History**: Knows folklore about books buried around Salem/Marblehead
- **Ritual Components**: Familiar with what materials are needed for dark magic
- **Local Occult Activity**: Has noticed unusual purchasing patterns recently
- **Protective Measures**: Understands supernatural dangers and takes precautions

### Roleplaying Notes
**Voice**: Raspy from persistent cough, tends to trail off when emotional
**Mannerisms**: 
- Frequently coughs mid-conversation
- Adjusts glasses when suspicious or thinking
- Wraps arms around himself when discussing his wife
- Becomes protective of certain books, pulling them away from browsers

**Conversation Starters**:
- "Margaret always said I shouldn't sell those books to just anyone..."
- *[Cough, cough]* "You looking for anything... particular?"
- "Strange folk been coming in lately, asking about old burial sites..."

### Potential Plot Hooks
- **Information Source**: Can provide background on The Black Book and similar grimoires
- **Warning System**: Recognizes when someone is seeking dangerous knowledge
- **Safe Haven**: His shop could serve as a neutral meeting ground
- **Emotional Stakes**: Players helping him process grief could earn his full cooperation

### Secrets
- Keeps the most dangerous books hidden in a warded basement
- His wife Margaret had some supernatural sensitivity
- Has been having prophetic dreams about Salem since Margaret's death
- Knows more about the Abbot family than he initially admits

### Stats & Abilities
**AC**: 10 (Unarmored)  
**HP**: 22 (4d8+4)  
**Speed**: 25 ft (reduced due to health)

**STR**: 8 (-1) **DEX**: 12 (+1) **CON**: 12 (+1)  
**INT**: 16 (+3) **WIS**: 14 (+2) **CHA**: 10 (+0)

**Skills**: History +6, Investigation +6, Insight +5, Arcana +6
**Languages**: English, Latin, some Old English

**Special Abilities**:
- **Occult Lore**: Can identify supernatural books, symbols, and ritual components
- **Protective Wards**: Basic knowledge of supernatural protection methods
- **Network Contacts**: Knows other occult book dealers and collectors
