# Grimtooth
## The Observant Malk

### Physical Description
Grimtooth appears to be a large Maine Coon cat with distinctive striped markings in shades of gray, black, and silver. He weighs approximately 20 pounds with a muscular build that suggests both comfort and capability. His fur is thick and well-maintained, and his eyes are an unusual amber-gold color that seem to hold far more intelligence than any normal cat should possess. His most striking feature is a small notch in his left ear—evidence of some past adventure or conflict.

**Most Notable Feature**: His eyes, which clearly show intelligent thought processes and occasionally seem to be evaluating situations with human-like reasoning.

### Personality Traits
- **Intellectually Curious**: Investigates interesting situations purely for knowledge
- **Pragmatically Neutral**: Takes no sides unless his own interests are threatened
- **Observational**: Prefers watching and learning to direct involvement
- **Independent**: Makes decisions based on his own priorities, not others' expectations
- **Quietly Judgmental**: Forms opinions about humans based on their actions

### Background
Grimtooth is a malk—a supernatural cat with human-level intelligence and the ability to speak. Like all malks, he's long-lived and possesses knowledge that spans decades. He arrived in the Salem area several months ago, drawn by the increasing supernatural activity. His decision to follow Henry Abbot stems from pure curiosity about the young man's activities rather than any moral judgment about their appropriateness.

### Current Situation
- **Active Surveillance**: Has been following Henry Abbot for months
- **Information Gathering**: Observes Coven activities without participating
- **Neutral Position**: Uninvolved in the conflict but possessing crucial intelligence
- **Potential Ally**: Could be convinced to share information under the right circumstances

### Malk Nature and Abilities
**Intelligence**: Human-level reasoning and problem-solving capabilities
**Communication**: Can speak any language he's heard enough to understand
**Supernatural Senses**: Enhanced perception of magical activities and supernatural entities
**Longevity**: Lifespan measured in centuries rather than decades
**Independence**: Not bound to serve humans but may choose to cooperate

### Surveillance Activities
Grimtooth has been systematically observing:
- **Henry's Daily Routine**: Knows his schedule at Salem State University and home
- **Coven Meetings**: Has watched multiple ritual gatherings from hidden positions
- **Winter Island Activities**: Familiar with the abandoned estate's layout and usage
- **Social Connections**: Understands the relationships between Coven members
- **Ritual Timing**: Has observed the patterns of their ceremonial activities

### Why He Follows Henry
**Initial Interest**: Henry's discovery of The Black Book created unusual magical disturbances
**Escalating Curiosity**: The increasing supernatural activity around Henry intrigued him
**Academic Interest**: Fascinated by human responses to supernatural power
**Non-Intervention Policy**: Prefers to observe rather than interfere with human affairs
**Personal Safety**: Maintains distance to avoid being affected by the Coven's activities

### Knowledge Base
**Coven Operations**: Detailed understanding of their methods and membership
**Henry's Psychology**: Insights into Henry's motivations and mental state
**Ritual Locations**: Knows all the sites used for ceremonies
**Timeline Information**: Can provide specific dates and times of past activities
**Future Plans**: Has overheard discussions about Halloween preparations

### Cat Advantages
**Stealth**: Can access areas and observe situations impossible for humans
**Ignored Presence**: Humans rarely pay attention to cats, allowing unnoticed surveillance
**Enhanced Senses**: Can detect magical residue, supernatural entities, and emotional states
**Mobility**: Able to follow targets across various terrains and buildings
**Night Vision**: Perfect surveillance capabilities in low-light conditions

### Personality Quirks
**Feline Behavior**: Despite his intelligence, still exhibits typical cat behaviors
**Selective Communication**: Chooses carefully when and to whom he reveals his speaking ability
**Comfort Seeking**: Prefers warm, comfortable locations for extended observations
**Food Motivated**: Appreciates good food and may trade information for quality meals
**Territorial Awareness**: Understands and respects other supernatural entities' territories

### Roleplaying Notes
**Voice**: Cultured, slightly sardonic, with the tone of someone who's seen everything
**Mannerisms**:
- Sits with perfect feline posture while speaking
- Occasionally pauses conversations to groom himself
- Maintains intense eye contact during important discussions
- Shows typical cat curiosity about new people and situations

**Speech Patterns**:
- "In my considerable experience observing humans..."
- "The young one grows more reckless with each passing moon."
- "I have no stake in your conflicts, but I find them... educational."

### Information Trading
Grimtooth might share intelligence in exchange for:
- **High-quality food**: Fresh fish, premium cat food, cream
- **Comfortable accommodations**: Warm places to sleep during winter
- **Intellectual stimulation**: Interesting conversations or puzzles
- **Mutual respect**: Treatment as an equal rather than a pet
- **Future favors**: Agreements for later assistance or protection

### Moral Stance
**Neutral Observer**: Doesn't judge human actions by human moral standards
**Pragmatic Ethics**: Concerned primarily with maintaining supernatural balance
**Non-Intervention**: Believes in allowing events to unfold naturally
**Self-Preservation**: Will act if his own safety becomes threatened
**Intellectual Honesty**: Provides accurate information without emotional bias

### Potential Plot Hooks
- **Intelligence Source**: Detailed knowledge of Coven activities and plans
- **Henry's Location**: Can lead characters directly to Henry when needed
- **Winter Island Guide**: Knows safe approaches and hidden entrances
- **Pattern Recognition**: Can identify changes in Coven behavior or escalation
- **Supernatural Detector**: His senses can identify magical threats others might miss

### Character Arc Opportunities
- Choosing to become actively involved rather than just observing
- Using his knowledge to guide inexperienced supernatural investigators
- Confronting other supernatural entities who threaten the area's balance
- Developing relationships with humans despite his preference for independence

### Stats & Abilities
**AC**: 14 (Natural Agility)
**HP**: 45 (10d8+10)
**Speed**: 40 ft, climb 30 ft

**STR**: 12 (+1) **DEX**: 18 (+4) **CON**: 12 (+1)
**INT**: 16 (+3) **WIS**: 18 (+4) **CHA**: 14 (+2)

**Skills**: Perception +7, Stealth +10, Investigation +6, Insight +7
**Senses**: Darkvision 60 ft, passive Perception 17
**Languages**: English, understands several others from eavesdropping
**Damage Resistances**: Psychic

**Special Abilities**:
- **Keen Senses**: Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight, hearing, or smell
- **Supernatural Awareness**: Can detect magical auras and supernatural entities within 60 feet
- **Feline Agility**: Can move through spaces as narrow as 1 foot without squeezing
- **Silent Movement**: Can move stealthily even across creaky floors or debris
- **Human Speech**: Can communicate in any language he's heard for at least a week

**Malk Traits**:
- **Enhanced Intelligence**: Retains perfect memory of observed events
- **Magical Resistance**: Advantage on saving throws against spells and magical effects
- **Longevity**: Ages much slower than normal cats
- **Supernatural Senses**: Can see invisible creatures and detect illusions
