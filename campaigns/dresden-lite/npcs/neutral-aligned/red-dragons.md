- # The Red Dragons
- ## Anti-Trafficking Gang

- ### Organization Overview
		- The Red Dragons are a primarily Chinese gang operating out of [[Boston]]'s Chinatown district. Unlike many criminal organizations, they have taken a principled stance against human trafficking and actively work to intercept and disrupt trafficking operations throughout the North Shore. While they engage in illegal activities like bootleg media sales and drug smuggling, they maintain strict ethical boundaries regarding human exploitation.

- ### Leadership Structure
		- **Dragon Head (Leader)**: Li Wei Chen - A man in his 50s with traditional values and modern business sense
		- **Lieutenant**: Susan Chang - Handles day-to-day operations and intelligence gathering
		- **Street Captain**: Marcus Wu - Coordinates field operations and rescue missions
		- **Business Manager**: David Huang - Manages legitimate front businesses including Hong Kong Kings

- ### Physical Presence
		- **Members**: Approximately 15-20 active members ranging from college age to middle-aged
		- **Appearance**: Mix of traditional Chinese gang aesthetics with modern urban styling
		- **Identification**: Subtle red dragon tattoos, specific jade jewelry, coded clothing colors
		- **Demeanor**: Professional criminals with strong moral convictions about protecting innocents

- ### Territory and Operations
		- **Primary Territory**: [[Boston]] Chinatown, with operations extending to [[Salem]] area
		- **Legitimate Businesses**: Hong Kong Kings restaurant, import/export companies, electronics repair shops
		- **Illegal Activities**: Bootleg media distribution, selective drug smuggling, occasional protection rackets
		- **Moral Boundaries**: Absolute prohibition on human trafficking, prostitution, or harm to innocents

- ### Anti-Trafficking Mission
		- **Intelligence Network**: Extensive contacts in shipping, trucking, and hospitality industries
		- **Rescue Operations**: Active interdiction of known trafficking routes and shipments
		- **Victim Support**: Provides safe houses, medical care, and relocation assistance
		- **Law Enforcement**: Selective cooperation with honest cops while avoiding corrupt officials

- ### Hong Kong Kings Restaurant
		- **Location**: Just off Essex Street, walking distance from main Chinatown
		- **Owner**: Li Wei Chen (Red Dragons leader)
		- **Manager**: David Huang (gang business manager)
		- **Employee**: Missy Lee (waitress, unaware of gang connections to anti-trafficking)
		- **Function**: Legitimate business that also serves as intelligence hub and safe meeting place

- ### Current Situation
		- **Trafficking Awareness**: Investigating increased human trafficking connected to Jared Larenzo
		- **Supernatural Ignorance**: Unaware of supernatural aspects of current crisis
		- **Resource Strain**: Limited manpower for both business operations and rescue missions
		- **Law Enforcement Pressure**: Increased police attention due to recent disappearances

- ### Gang Operations
		- **Bootleg Media Business**:
- **Products**: Pirated DVDs, Blu-rays, streaming device modifications
- **Distribution**: Throughout [[Boston]] area, primarily to working-class customers
- **Profits**: Moderate income that funds anti-trafficking operations

- **Selective Drug Smuggling**:
- **Products**: Primarily marijuana, some prescription medications
- **Ethics**: Refuses to handle hard drugs or anything targeting minors
- **Markets**: Adult customers only, with emphasis on medical users

- **Protection Services**:
- **Clients**: Legitimate businesses in Chinatown facing extortion
- **Methods**: Non-violent deterrence, community organizing, selective enforcement
- **Philosophy**: Protecting community rather than exploiting it

- ### Anti-Trafficking Methods
		- **Intelligence Gathering**: Network of informants in shipping, hospitality, and transport industries
		- **Route Interdiction**: Identifying and disrupting trafficking shipments and movements
		- **Victim Rescue**: Direct intervention to free trafficked individuals
		- **Safe House Network**: Temporary shelters and support services for rescued victims
		- **Relocation Services**: Helping victims establish new identities and lives

- ### Key Personnel
		- **Li Wei Chen (Dragon Head)**:
- **Age**: 54, traditional businessman appearance
- **Background**: Immigrated from Hong Kong, lost family members to trafficking
- **Motivation**: Personal vendetta against human traffickers
- **Skills**: Strategic planning, community organizing, traditional martial arts

- **Susan Chang (Lieutenant)**:
- **Age**: 35, professional businesswoman demeanor
- **Background**: Former social worker who turned to gang life after system failures
- **Role**: Intelligence coordination, victim support services
- **Skills**: Investigation, counseling, multilingual communication

- **Marcus Wu (Street Captain)**:
- **Age**: 28, athletic build with military bearing
- **Background**: Former Marine who joined gang after discharge
- **Role**: Field operations, security, direct action missions
- **Skills**: Combat tactics, firearms, urban warfare

- ### Relationship with Jared Larenzo
		- **Antagonistic**: The Red Dragons are actively investigating Larenzo's trafficking operation
		- **Intelligence Target**: Trying to gather evidence of his activities and connections
		- **Rescue Focus**: Attempting to intercept his shipments and free victims
		- **Unaware of Supernatural Aspects**: Don't understand the magical components of his business

- ### Community Relations
		- **Chinatown Protection**: Serve as informal community protectors
		- **Business Support**: Help legitimate businesses resist criminal extortion
		- **Cultural Preservation**: Maintain traditional Chinese cultural practices and values
		- **Youth Outreach**: Provide alternatives to gang life for at-risk teenagers

- ### Roleplaying Notes
		- **Communication Style**: Mix of business formality and street pragmatism
		- **Language**: English with occasional Mandarin/Cantonese phrases
		- **Values**: Strong emphasis on family, community, and protecting innocents
		- **Methods**: Professional criminals with strict moral codes

- **Typical Interactions**:
- "We handle our business quietly, but trafficking crosses lines we don't tolerate."
- "The restaurant serves good food and asks no questions about private conversations."
- "Some people think all gangs are the same. We show them the difference."
