- # Daniel Fairview
- ## The Ancient Businessman

- ### Physical Description
		- Daniel appears to be a distinguished man in his early 40s with the polished appearance of old money and careful grooming. He has dark hair with distinguished silver at the temples, pale skin that never seems to tan, and piercing dark eyes that seem to look right through people. His build is lean and elegant, and he moves with the fluid grace of someone completely comfortable in their own skin. He dresses impeccably in tailored suits and high-quality accessories, projecting an image of successful sophistication.

- **Most Notable Feature**: His complete stillness when not actively engaged—he doesn't fidget, breathe visibly, or make unconscious movements that living humans do.

- ### Personality Traits
- **Business-Minded**: Approaches most interactions as potential transactions
- **Pragmatically Amoral**: Not evil, but completely unconcerned with conventional morality
- **Sophisticated**: Centuries of experience have refined his tastes and manners
- **Calculating**: Every decision is made based on cost-benefit analysis
- **Survival-Focused**: His primary concern is maintaining his existence and comfort

- ### Background
		- Daniel Fairview was born in the 1400s and has lived through the rise and fall of empires. He came to the New World relatively early in European colonization, drawn by the opportunities of a developing continent. Over the centuries, he's built substantial wealth through careful investments, property acquisition, and business ventures. Unlike many vampires who struggle with modernity, Daniel has embraced capitalism and uses it as a feeding system—both literally and figuratively.

- ### Current Situation
- **Business Empire**: Owns multiple properties and businesses throughout [[Salem]]
- **Feeding Network**: Has established sustainable feeding practices
- **Investment Interest**: Financially involved with Jared Larenzo's trafficking operation
- **Neutral Position**: Avoids involvement in supernatural politics unless profits are threatened

- ### Vampire Nature
		- **Age**: Approximately 600 years old
		- **Feeding**: Requires human blood but has developed sustainable methods
		- **Limitations**: Traditional vampire weaknesses (sunlight, running water, holy symbols)
		- **Advantages**: Enhanced strength, speed, senses, and centuries of accumulated knowledge
		- **Appearance**: Looks like a man in his early 40s, will never visibly age

- ### Business Holdings
		- **Harbor Light Hotel & Restaurant**:
- **Location**: Prime waterfront location in [[Salem]]
- **Services**: Upscale accommodations, fine dining, event hosting
- **Clientele**: Wealthy tourists, business travelers, supernatural-aware individuals
- **Income**: Substantial profits that fund his other ventures

- **Old Butcher Shop**:
- **Location**: Converted property in [[Salem]]'s historic district
- **Current Use**: Venue for exclusive "vampire blood raves"
- **Clientele**: Vampires and supernatural entities seeking social gatherings
- **Security**: Heavily warded and secured against intrusion

- **Additional Properties**:
- Several rental properties throughout [[Salem]] and [[Boston]]
- Commercial real estate investments
- Stock portfolios managed through various identities

- ### The Blood Rave Operation
		- Daniel hosts exclusive gatherings for [[Salem]]'s vampire community:
- **Frequency**: Monthly events during new moon periods
- **Participants**: Local vampires, visiting supernatural entities, select human "donors"
- **Activities**: Social networking, feeding opportunities, information exchange
- **Rules**: Strict no-killing policy, consensual feeding only, absolute discretion

- ### Feeding Practices
		- Daniel has developed ethical (by vampire standards) feeding methods:
- **Willing Donors**: Pays humans for blood donations
- **Medical Connections**: Sources blood from hospitals and blood banks
- **Non-Lethal**: Has not killed for feeding in over a century
- **Discrete**: Maintains the Masquerade through careful victim selection

- ### Business Relationship with Jared Larenzo
		- **Investment Nature**: Provides financial backing for trafficking operations
		- **Motivation**: Profits from both the money and access to victims
		- **Ethical Boundary**: Insists on "no unnecessary killing" policies
		- **Business Distance**: Maintains plausible deniability about operational details

- ### Knowledge and Information
		- **Supernatural Community**: Knows most supernatural entities in the North Shore area
		- **Historical Context**: Understands the long-term patterns of supernatural activity
		- **Business Intelligence**: Has access to financial records and property information
		- **Network Access**: Can facilitate meetings between different supernatural factions

- ### Stance on the Coven Crisis
		- **Position**: Concerned but not directly involved
		- **Business Impact**: Increased law enforcement attention threatens his operations
		- **Practical Concerns**: Mass exposure would destroy the supernatural economy
		- **Potential Action**: Might provide resources if properly compensated

- ### Roleplaying Notes
		- **Voice**: Cultured, slightly archaic speech patterns, speaks multiple languages
		- **Mannerisms**:
- Perfect posture and controlled movements
- Never eats or drinks in public (claims dietary restrictions)
- Checks expensive pocket watch frequently
- Speaks about current events with historical perspective

- **Conversation Starters**:
- "In my experience, most problems can be solved with the right... investment."
- "I've seen this sort of chaos before. It never ends well for business."
- "Time is money, as they say, and I've had considerable time to accumulate both."

- ### Character Motivations
		- **Primary Goal**: Maintain his comfortable lifestyle and business empire
		- **Secondary Goal**: Preserve the supernatural community's secrecy
		- **Fears**: Exposure, economic collapse, losing his established territory
		- **Desires**: Continued profits, social stability, respect from peers

- ### Potential Plot Hooks
- **Information Broker**: Can provide intelligence about supernatural community
- **Resource Provider**: Able to fund operations or provide safe houses
- **Neutral Meeting Ground**: His hotel serves as neutral territory for negotiations
- **Historical Knowledge**: Understands patterns that might predict Coven behavior

- ### Moral Complexity
		- Daniel operates in ethical gray areas:
- **Supernatural Predator**: Requires human blood to survive
- **Ethical Feeding**: Has developed non-lethal feeding practices
- **Business Criminal**: Involved in human trafficking for profit
- **Community Stabilizer**: His businesses provide structure for supernatural society

- ### Character Arc Opportunities
- Choosing between profit and community safety
- Confronting the consequences of his business partnerships
- Using his resources to actively oppose the Coven
- Revealing hidden aspects of [[Salem]]'s supernatural history

- ### Stats & Abilities
		- **AC**: 16 (Natural Armor)
		- **HP**: 144 (17d8+68)
		- **Speed**: 30 ft

- **STR**: 18 (+4) **DEX**: 18 (+4) **CON**: 18 (+4)
- **INT**: 17 (+3) **WIS**: 15 (+2) **CHA**: 18 (+4)

- **Saving Throws**: Dex +9, Wis +7, Cha +9
- **Skills**: Deception +9, Insight +7, Perception +7, Persuasion +9, Stealth +9
- **Damage Resistances**: Necrotic; bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks
- **Languages**: English, French, Spanish, Italian, Latin, German

- **Special Abilities**:
- **Vampire Traits**: Legendary actions, regeneration, charm, shapechage
- **Business Network**: Extensive contacts in legitimate and criminal enterprises
- **Centuries of Experience**: Advantage on History checks and social situations
- **Supernatural Senses**: Can detect supernatural entities and magical activity

- **Legendary Actions** (3 per turn):
- Move without provoking opportunity attacks
- Make one unarmed strike
- Cast charm person (costs 2 actions)

- **Weaknesses**:
- **Sunlight Hypersensitivity**: Disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks in sunlight
- **Stake to Heart**: Paralyzed if pierced through heart with wooden stake
- **Running Water**: Cannot cross flowing water except at low tide or bridges
