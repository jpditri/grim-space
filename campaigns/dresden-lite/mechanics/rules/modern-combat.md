- # Modern Combat & Firearms Rules

- ## Firearms Mechanics

	- ### Basic Firearms
- **Attack Roll**: Standard D20 + ability modifier + proficiency bonus
- **Damage**: As per weapon statistics
- **Range**: Standard firearm ranges apply

	- ### Automatic Weapons
- **Range Penalty**: Disadvantage on attacks beyond 30 feet unless character has firearms training
- **Training Requirement**: Military, law enforcement, or specialized firearms training background
- **Hit Determination**: D4 roll for number of bullets that hit target (only if initial D20 attack succeeds)

	- ### Automatic Weapons Hit System
- 1. Roll D20 to determine if attack hits
- 2. If hit, roll D4 to determine number of bullets that connect
- 3. Apply damage for each bullet hit
- 4. Consider cover, armor, and defensive positioning

	- ### Weapon Concealment
- **Open Carry Restrictions**: Carrying weapons openly draws immediate police attention
- **Concealment Methods**: 
  - Shoulder holsters for pistols
  - Briefcases or guitar cases for larger weapons
  - Tool disguises for improvised weapons
- **Legal Consequences**: Weapon violations result in arrest and investigation
- **Supernatural Weapons**: Even more suspicious to authorities

- ## Magic Concealment Rules

	- ### The Masquerade
- **No Open Magic**: Casting spells in public causes mass panic
- **Witness Reactions**: Civilians will flee, call police, record video
- **Official Response**: Authorities create conventional explanations
- **Cover Stories**: Explosions become "gas leaks," disappearances become "missing persons"

	- ### Consequence Escalation
- 1. **First Exposure**: Treated as isolated incident, covered up locally
- 2. **Repeated Exposure**: Federal investigation begins
- 3. **Major Exposure**: National security response, manhunt initiated
- 4. **Catastrophic Exposure**: Military intervention, supernatural community mobilizes

	- ### Stealth Casting
- **Subtle Spell Techniques**: Metamagic to reduce visible effects
- **Environmental Cover**: Use weather, darkness, or crowds to mask casting
- **Misdirection**: Make magic appear technological or coincidental
- **Sacred Spaces**: Some locations have supernatural concealment

- ## Law Enforcement Response

	- ### Police Escalation
- **Beat Cops**: First responders, rely on conventional explanations
- **Detectives**: Investigate patterns, may suspect supernatural
- **Federal Agents**: Have resources to track supernatural activity
- **Special Task Forces**: Elite units aware of supernatural threats

	- ### Investigation Procedures
- **Evidence Collection**: Supernatural evidence often dismissed or explained away
- **Witness Interviews**: Traumatized witnesses often discredited
- **Media Management**: Stories suppressed or spun as hoaxes
- **Inter-Agency Cooperation**: Different levels of supernatural awareness

- ## Character Restrictions

	- ### Race Limitations
- **Humans Only**: All player characters must be human
- **Supernatural Heritage**: Some humans may have dormant supernatural bloodlines
- **Awakened Humans**: Normal humans who become aware of supernatural world

	- ### Class Restrictions
- **No Artificer**: Technology-based magic doesn't fit setting theme
- **Alternative Options**: Tech-savvy characters use mundane technology enhanced by supernatural knowledge

- ## Social Dynamics

	- ### The Supernatural Community
- **Hidden Society**: Supernatural beings maintain secrecy from mundane world
- **Enforcement**: Supernatural police monitor exposure violations
- **Punishment**: Beings who expose the supernatural face exile or worse
- **Alliances**: Supernatural factions work together to maintain masquerade

	- ### Mundane World Integration
- **Day Jobs**: Characters need normal employment as cover
- **Social Connections**: Maintain relationships with unaware mundanes
- **Technology**: Modern tech complicates supernatural concealment
- **Social Media**: Digital evidence requires careful management
