# Boston Supernatural World - The Hidden Reality

## The Masquerade

In the greater Boston-North Shore area, the supernatural world exists in the shadows of everyday life. While the United States continues its familiar trajectory - China rising as a global power, corporations expanding influence, social media dominating communication - a parallel reality operates just beneath the surface.

### The Hidden Truth
- **Ghosts and Goblins**: Real supernatural beings hide among the mundane population
- **Dragons**: Extremely rare, often taking human form to blend into society
- **Supernatural Secrecy**: Beings must conceal their existence or face being hunted
- **Modern Folklore**: Old Grendelkin tales have basis in supernatural reality

### The Masquerade Rules
1. **No Open Magic**: Supernatural abilities cannot be displayed publicly
2. **Official Cover-Ups**: Authorities create conventional explanations for supernatural events
3. **Community Enforcement**: Supernatural beings police their own to maintain secrecy
4. **Severe Consequences**: Exposure violations face exile, hunting, or worse

## Geographic Setting

### Primary Territory: Boston-North Shore Massachusetts
The supernatural community operates throughout the greater Boston metropolitan area, with particular concentration in historically significant locations.

### Key Supernatural Locations

#### **Wonderland Station Revere**
- **Mundane Function**: Public transit hub
- **Supernatural Significance**: Neutral meeting ground for information exchange
- **Hidden Features**: Messages left in graffiti use supernatural codes

#### **TD Garden**
- **Mundane Function**: Major sports and entertainment venue
- **Supernatural Significance**: Large crowds provide cover for supernatural gatherings
- **Hidden Features**: Private boxes serve as faction meeting spaces

#### **China Town Boston**
- **Mundane Function**: Cultural district and tourist area
- **Supernatural Significance**: Historic supernatural community settlement
- **Hidden Features**: Traditional supernatural protections and hidden temples

#### **Essex Street Salem**
- **Mundane Function**: Historic witchcraft tourism area
- **Supernatural Significance**: Ironic cover for actual supernatural activity
- **Hidden Features**: Real practitioners hide among fake tourist attractions

#### **Lynn Woods Reservation**
- **Mundane Function**: 2,200-acre forest preserve
- **Supernatural Significance**: Neutral territory and supernatural sanctuary
- **Hidden Features**: Ancient pacts protect supernatural beings within the woods

#### **Marblehead Harbor Areas**
- **Mundane Function**: Affluent coastal communities
- **Supernatural Significance**: Old money families with supernatural connections
- **Hidden Features**: Maritime supernatural traditions and sea-based entities

### The Network

#### **Supernatural Establishments**
- **Riptide Bar Marblehead**: Supernatural-friendly establishment with coded entry
- **The Tavern on the Square Salem**: Information hub disguised as tourist restaurant
- **YMCA Salem**: Community programs that train young supernatural beings

#### **Information Centers**
- **Peabody Essex Museum**: Archives containing sanitized supernatural history
- **Private Collections**: Hidden supernatural artifacts and documentation
- **Underground Networks**: Communication systems between supernatural communities

## Supernatural Demographics

### Human Population
- **Unaware Majority**: 99% of humans remain ignorant of supernatural reality
- **Awakened Humans**: Small percentage who become aware through circumstances
- **Hunter Families**: Generational lines dedicated to supernatural elimination
- **Supernatural Allies**: Humans who support and protect supernatural beings

### Supernatural Community
- **Established Residents**: Beings who have lived in the area for generations
- **Recent Arrivals**: Supernatural refugees from other regions
- **Transient Population**: Beings passing through on supernatural business
- **Hidden Families**: Mixed supernatural-human households maintaining secrets

## Modern Challenges

### Technology Complications
- **Digital Evidence**: Cameras and recording devices make concealment harder
- **Social Media**: Viral videos can expose supernatural activity instantly
- **Surveillance**: Government and corporate monitoring systems detect anomalies
- **Communication**: Digital channels may be monitored by hostile forces

### Contemporary Threats
- **Corporate Interests**: Some corporations aware of and exploit supernatural assets
- **Government Agencies**: Federal task forces investigate unexplained phenomena
- **Extremist Groups**: Anti-supernatural organizations with modern resources
- **Internal Conflicts**: Supernatural faction disputes in urban environment

## Laws and Enforcement

### Human Authorities
- **Local Police**: Generally unaware, explain away supernatural incidents
- **Federal Agents**: Some agencies have supernatural awareness divisions
- **Special Task Forces**: Elite units specifically trained for supernatural threats
- **Military Elements**: Classified programs dealing with supernatural assets

### Supernatural Justice
- **Community Courts**: Hidden judicial systems for supernatural law
- **Enforcement Officers**: Supernatural beings maintaining community order
- **Punishment Systems**: Exile, binding, or elimination for masquerade violations
- **Appeal Processes**: Limited recourse for supernatural justice decisions
