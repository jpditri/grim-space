- # [[Saljaimer]] (Golden City)

Type: City (rich, independent)
Region: Outside Antolian Hills, southern border
Summary: Wealthy, opulent city run by [[King Shabaku]] and [[Queen Elsuon]]; known for its embassies, artists, and "Holy Street."
- Notable NPCs: [[King Shabaku]], [[Queen Elsuon]]
- Features:
- Powerful trade network, embassies, adventurers' haven
- Flourishing arts, festival and performance culture
- "Holy Street" district for religious visitors
- Strong orc army presence (former raiders now guards)
- Hooks:
- Source of political intrigue, treaties, mercenary recruitment
- Artistic quests and opportunities
