# Grimore

Type: City (Peaceful/Blessed)
Region: East of Mascas, south of Jorbundo
Summary: Home to Princess Katzen; famed for magical blessing, famous zoo, ring-shaped Great Garden, and cultural wonders.
Notable NPCs: Princess Katzen, Botan, Ikerdal
Features:
- Open zoo of magical beasts
- Expansive ring-shaped garden
- Largest church of Pelor in the world
- Both opulent and seedy districts
Hooks:
- Sanctuary for the troubled, source of unique animal-based encounters
- Center for magical or diplomatic questlines
