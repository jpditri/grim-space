- # [[King Shabaku]]
Race: Orc (Noble)
Class: Fighter (Commander)
Alignment: Lawful Neutral
Pronouns: He/Him
Location: [[Saljaimer]]
- Background:
- Once a chieftain, now King of the Orcs and ruler of [[Saljaimer]]. Appointed by King Halus, balances his people's needs with diplomacy and prosperity for all races. Open to alliances.
Traits: Determined, pragmatic, respectful of tradition
Hook: Controls one of the campaign’s wealthiest cities, moderates orc aggression, and might ally with/betray PCs depending on negotiation.
