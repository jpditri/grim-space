- # [[Coven Sisters]] (Brianna, Cassandra, Delilah)
Race: Human (possible fey ancestry)
Class: Wizards/Sorceresses
Alignment: Neutral Good
Pronouns: She/Her (all)
Location: [[Narrow Sectarian]]
- Background:
- [[Athena]]’s sisters Brianna, Cassandra, Delilah contribute to [[Narrow Sectarian]]’s defense via healing, enchantments, and ritual. Each is distinct in magical aptitude and community influence.
- Traits:
- Brianna: Empathic healer, cautious
- Cassandra: Visionary, insightful
- Delilah: Bold, experimenter
Hook: Gatherings of the sisters foretell major campaign events, and they can collectively bless or ward.
