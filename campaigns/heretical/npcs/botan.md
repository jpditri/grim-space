# Botan
Race: Elf
Class: Druid (Circle of the Land)
Alignment: Neutral Good
Pronouns: He/Him
Location: Grimore
Background:
  Botan, renowned Arborist of Cayabor, designed the Great Garden in Grimore. Known for his knowledge of both plants and magical nature rites.
Traits: Quiet, contemplative, attuned to nature
Hook: Can provide rare druidic insight, cures, or guides to the natural world and the “hidden paths.”
