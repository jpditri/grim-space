# King Everec
Race: Dwarf
Class: Fighter (Champion)
Alignment: Lawful Good
Pronouns: He/Him
Location: Mount Menastily
Background:
  Dwarven king who struck alliance with Narrow Sectarian via Athena. Supplies building materials and defensive works in exchange for bread and mead.
Traits: Stern, steadfast, generous to allies
Hook: May grant access to dwarven arms, construction, and secret roads/trade routes.
