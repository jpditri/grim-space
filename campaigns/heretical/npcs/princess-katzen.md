# Princess Katzen (The Purple Princess)
Race: Human
Class: Bard (Royal, Performer)
Alignment: Chaotic Good
Pronouns: She/Her
Location: Grimore
Background:
  Princess Katzen, famed for her purple hair, rules over Grimore, the magically-blessed city of peace and wonder. Respected for her openness and diplomacy, removed from Mascas by her uncle Krolus for political reasons.
Traits: Charismatic, artistic, benevolent, naturally magical
Hook: Can provide sanctuary, access to unique beasts, and magical blessings to PCs.
