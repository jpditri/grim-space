# Factions & Organizations: Heretical Campaign

## The Order of Krolus
Type: Knightly order / Military cult
Allegiance: Lord Krolus
Keywords: Oppression, fear, martial prowess, demon worship
Summary: Elite knights/agents for Lord Krolus. Tasked with enforcing his will, assassinations, kidnappings, terror. Feared across the Antolian Hills.
Primary Base: Mascas, Eliozor (now ruined)
Notable Members: [To be cross-linked with NPCs]

## Coven Sisters
Type: Arcane Circle
Summary: Athena, Brianna, Cassandra, and Delilah. Resident witches of Narrow Sectarian. Offer magical support, healing, defense, and magical items.
Primary Base: Narrow Sectarian
Notes: Athena is known for magic item imbue. Allied with King Everec (Dwarves).

## The Heretical
Type: Adventurers / Heroes
Summary: Once blamed for tragedy in Eliozor, defended the Antolian Hills from Krolus. Famous, controversial party; represent hope to many, suspicion to others.
Prominent Members: Trebor Nodrog, others [see NPCs]
Notes: Sometimes called "the upstart band," credited with major victories and failures.

## House of Blackrock Crow
Type: Vampire Society
Summary: Dangerous, enigmatic group of vampires, stronghold within Mascas. Rumored to be involved in political intrigue and forbidden magic.
Notes: Master is a fair-skinned dark-haired woman; close relationship with Lord Krolus suspected. Details secretive.

## The Cultists/Fanatics
Type: Cult
Allegiance: Various (many tied to Krolus or forbidden gods)
Summary: Large group of brainwashed minions, serve the evil Heretical/cult leaders. Seek to bring apocalypse or otherwise disrupt order.
Primary Base: Throughout Antolian Hills

## Ambassadors of Saljaimer
Type: Political/diplomatic council
Summary: Many ambassadors in Saljaimer drive trade and alliances between regions; critical to modern politics of the campaign.
