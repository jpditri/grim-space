# Locations: Heretical Campaign (D&D 5e Compatible)

## Narrow Sectarian
Type: Village
Summary: Diverse, fast-growing border town on the Eastern Antolian Hills noted for its churches, market, mead, and bread.
Notable NPCs: Athena (Coven Sisters), King Everec (Mountain Dwarves)
Features:
- Market, multiple faiths, major wheat and mead production, taverns
- Alliance with dwarves for defense/building

## Eliozor
Type: City (ruined/occupied)
Summary: Once a large city, homeland of Trebor Nodrog. Lost in the battle with the Order of Krolus.
Notable NPCs: Trebor Nodrog
Features:
- Burned in battle against Krolus's army
- Former base of Order of Krolus

## Mascas
Type: Capital City
Summary: Once-glorious capital, now subject to Lord Krolus's rule and decay.
Notable NPCs: Lord Krolus, (Princess) Katzen
Features:
- Proximity to Grimore, Orinda, Saljaimer, Goblin Mountain
- Center of political intrigue under dark rule

## Estonia
Type: Fortified City
Summary: Oldest city, a pilgrimage site and former capital; known for peace and strong markets.
Notable NPCs: --
Features:
- Many religions and ancient temples
- Strong defensive works

## Ice Goblin Mountain
Type: Wilderness/Dungeon
Summary: Dangerous, goblin-infested mountain surrounded by trade roads and ambush sites.
Notable NPCs: Goblin leaders (unnamed)
Features: Frequent raids, difficult travel

## Saljaimer (Golden City)
Type: City (rich, independent)
Summary: Opulent city run by King Shabaku, famed for its artists, politics, markets, "Holy Street," and orc/diplomat intrigue.
Notable NPCs: King Shabaku, Queen Elsuon
Features:
- Refuge for adventurers, ambassadors' council, thriving arts
- Artist hub, sanctuary for travelers, embassies

## Grimore
Type: City (Peaceful/Blessed)
Summary: Ruled by Princess Katzen, magically warded with a famous zoo, grand gardens, and sites for tourism and healing.
Notable NPCs: Princess Katzen, Botan, Ikerdal
Features:
- Open zoo, Great Garden, largest church of Pelor, crime districts on city rim

## Jorbundo
Type: City (former academia/innovation)
Summary: Once center of intellectual and technological progress, now tragically collapsed into ruin and horror.
Notable NPCs: -- (engineers, headmistresses, etc.)
Features:
- Site of "Six Banners" amusement park, now overrun by cannibalism after societal collapse
