# Resonance Spider

*Medium beast, unaligned*

**Armor Class** 15 (Natural Armor)  
**Hit Points** 68 (8d8 + 32)  
**Speed** 30 ft., climb 30 ft.

## Ability Scores
| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 14  | 17  | 18  | 4   | 14  | 8   |

**Skills** Perception +4, Stealth +7  
**Damage Resistances** Psychic, Thunder  
**Senses** Blindsight 60 ft., Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 14  
**Languages** —  
**Challenge Rating** 4 (1,100 XP)

## Special Abilities

**Crystal-Web Weaving.** The spider can create webs made from crystalline silk that resonates with xenocortex frequencies. These webs are invisible to normal sight but glow faintly to Vaarn with active xenocortices.

**Xenocortex Sensitivity.** The spider can detect active xenocortices within 500 feet and is drawn to areas of high xenocortex activity. It can sense the emotional state of creatures with xenocortices.

**Harmonic Disruption.** When the spider takes damage from a creature with a xenocortex, it releases a discordant frequency. The attacker must make a DC 14 Wisdom saving throw or have their xenocortex temporarily disrupted, losing access to one stored pattern until they complete a short rest.

**Web Walker.** The spider ignores movement restrictions caused by webbing and can move through its crystal webs without triggering their resonance effects.

## Actions

**Multiattack.** The spider makes two attacks: one with its bite and one with its crystalline claws.

**Bite.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. *Hit:* 8 (1d8 + 4) piercing damage plus 9 (2d8) psychic damage. If the target has a xenocortex, it must make a DC 14 Constitution saving throw or be paralyzed for 1 minute as its xenocortex becomes overwhelmed by the spider's venom. The target can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.

**Crystalline Claws.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 11 (2d6 + 4) slashing damage. If the target is wearing metal armor or wielding metal weapons, they become magnetized to nearby crystal formations for 1 minute.

**Crystal Web (Recharge 5-6).** The spider shoots crystalline webbing at a point within 30 feet. The web creates a 20-foot radius area of difficult terrain. Any creature that starts its turn in the web or enters it during its turn must make a DC 14 Dexterity saving throw or be restrained. A restrained creature can use its action to make a DC 14 Strength check, freeing itself on a success. The web has AC 12, 15 hit points, and is vulnerable to thunder damage. Creatures with active xenocortices take 4 (1d8) psychic damage each turn they remain in the web.

**Resonance Screech (1/Day).** The spider emits a piercing crystalline shriek. All creatures within 30 feet must make a DC 14 Constitution saving throw. On a failure, creatures take 18 (4d8) thunder damage and are deafened for 1 minute. Creatures with xenocortices make this save with disadvantage and on a failure also have their stored patterns scrambled - they cannot access any stored patterns for 24 hours.

## Lore and Ecology

**Origin:** Resonance Spiders evolved on Kepharion in areas of high crystalline activity. Over millennia, they developed the ability to sense and interact with xenocortex frequencies, making them both fascinating and dangerous to Vaarn populations.

**Behavior:** These creatures are not inherently hostile but are highly territorial around crystal formations. They are attracted to Vaarn settlements because of the concentrated xenocortex activity, often leading to dangerous encounters. They seem to feed on some form of energy emitted by active xenocortices.

**Habitat:** Commonly found in:
- Crystal caves and underground formations
- Areas around Vaarn settlements with high xenocortex activity
- Ancient ruins where crystalline technology was once prevalent
- Near xenocortex archive sites

**Life Cycle:** Resonance Spiders lay their eggs in crystalline formations, and the young feed on crystal residue until they develop their own resonance abilities. Mature spiders can live for decades and become increasingly attuned to xenocortex frequencies over time.

**Hunting Patterns:** They prefer to set crystal-web traps near paths frequented by Vaarn. They can sense approaching xenocortices from great distances and will position themselves accordingly. They typically avoid groups of Vaarn unless extremely hungry or protecting their territory.

## Combat Tactics

**Ambush Predator:** Uses its crystal webs to create nearly invisible traps in areas where Vaarn frequently travel. The webs are positioned to funnel victims toward the spider's hiding spot.

**Xenocortex Targeting:** Prioritizes attacking creatures with xenocortices, using its bite to paralyze them before dragging them away. Against non-Vaarn creatures, it relies more on its claws and webs.

**Territorial Defense:** When protecting its lair or eggs, the spider will use its Resonance Screech early in combat to disorient multiple attackers, then retreat to prepared web defenses.

## Treasure

**Crystal Silk Glands:** Worth 200 gp to alchemists studying xenocortex enhancement
**Resonance Crystals:** Small crystals from the spider's body can be used to craft xenocortex tuning devices (50 gp each, 1d4 crystals per spider)
**Web Material:** Harvested crystal silk can be woven into items that provide +1 bonus to saves against psychic effects

## Campaign Integration

### Plot Hooks
- Resonance Spiders have infested the path to an important xenocortex archive
- A Vaarn settlement is being terrorized by spiders drawn to a powerful xenocortex artifact
- Players must capture a live specimen for a researcher studying xenocortex-creature interactions
- Spider infestations indicate the presence of valuable crystal formations

### Environmental Hazards
- Areas with resonance spider activity often have unstable crystal formations
- Their webs can interfere with xenocortex-based navigation and communication
- Large populations may indicate an underlying disruption in local xenocortex frequencies
