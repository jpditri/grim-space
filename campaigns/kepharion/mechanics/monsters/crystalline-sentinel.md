- # Crystalline Sentinel

- *Large construct, lawful neutral*

- **Armor Class** 17 (Natural Armor)
- **Hit Points** 142 (15d12 + 45)
- **Speed** 30 ft., burrow 20 ft.

- ## Ability Scores
	- | STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
	- |-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
	- | 20  | 8   | 16  | 14  | 15  | 6   |

- **Saving Throws** Con +9, Wis +8
- **Skills** Perception +8, Investigation +8
- **Damage Resistances** Cold, Lightning; Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing from Nonmagical Attacks
- **Damage Immunities** Poison, Psychic
- **Condition Immunities** Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned
- **Senses** Tremorsense 60 ft., Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 18
- **Languages** Understands Vaarnish and Common but can't speak
- **Challenge Rating** 8 (3,900 XP)

- ## Special Abilities

- **[[Xenocortex]] Resonance.** The sentinel can detect and communicate with xenocortices within 1 mile. Vaarn creatures within 60 feet have advantage on Intelligence and Wisdom saving throws.

- **Crystal Network.** If multiple crystalline sentinels are within 1 mile of each other, they share sensory information and can coordinate actions perfectly.

- **Harmonic Disruption.** When the sentinel takes damage, it emits a discordant crystal frequency. All creatures within 30 feet must make a DC 16 Constitution saving throw or be stunned until the end of their next turn. Vaarn creatures make this save with disadvantage.

- **Magic Resistance.** The sentinel has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

- **Immutable Form.** The sentinel is immune to any spell or effect that would alter its form.

- ## Actions

- **Multiattack.** The sentinel makes two crystal slam attacks or uses its Resonance Beam twice.

- **Crystal Slam.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +11 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 18 (2d12 + 5) bludgeoning damage plus 9 (2d8) thunder damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a DC 17 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.

- **Resonance Beam.** *Ranged Spell Attack:* +8 to hit, range 120 ft., one creature. *Hit:* 22 (4d8 + 4) psychic damage. If the target has a [[xenocortex]], it takes an additional 11 (2d10) psychic damage and must make a DC 16 Wisdom saving throw or have one stored pattern corrupted.

- **Crystal Spike Eruption (Recharge 5-6).** The sentinel causes crystal spikes to erupt from the ground in a 30-foot radius around it. All creatures in the area must make a DC 17 Dexterity saving throw, taking 27 (6d8) piercing damage on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one. The area becomes difficult terrain until cleared.

- **[[Xenocortex]] Override (1/Day).** The sentinel attempts to temporarily control a Vaarn creature within 60 feet. The target must make a DC 16 Charisma saving throw. On a failure, the sentinel controls the target's actions for up to 1 minute. The target can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success.

- ## Legendary Actions (3 per turn)
	- The sentinel can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn.

- **Move.** The sentinel moves up to its speed without provoking opportunity attacks.
- **Crystal Slam.** The sentinel makes one crystal slam attack.
- **Harmonic Pulse.** All creatures within 30 feet must make a DC 16 Constitution saving throw or be deafened until the end of their next turn.

- ## Lore and Ecology

- **Origin:** Crystalline Sentinels are ancient constructs created by early Vaarn civilizations to guard important [[xenocortex]] archives and crystal formations. They were originally designed to be protectors and caretakers of crystalline knowledge repositories.

- **Behavior:** These constructs are not malevolent but are extremely protective of their assigned territories. They will attempt to communicate with Vaarn through [[xenocortex]] resonance before becoming hostile, but will defend their posts against any perceived threats.

- **Habitat:** Found primarily in:
- Ancient [[xenocortex]] archive sites
- Natural crystal cave systems
- Abandoned Vaarn settlements with significant crystalline infrastructure
- Underground networks connecting multiple crystal formations

- **Tactics:** Crystalline Sentinels prefer to use their Resonance Beam against Vaarn to disrupt their [[xenocortex]] functions, while using physical attacks against non-Vaarn creatures. They coordinate perfectly with other sentinels in the area and will use terrain to their advantage.

- **Treasure:** When defeated, a Crystalline Sentinel's core crystal can be harvested (worth 2,500 gp) and used to create [[xenocortex]] enhancement devices. The sentinel may also be guarding ancient Vaarn artifacts or stored memory crystals.

- ## Campaign Integration

- ### Plot Hooks
- Ancient archive protected by sentinels contains crucial historical information
- Malfunctioning sentinel is corrupting xenocortices in nearby Vaarn settlement
- Players must negotiate with sentinels to access forbidden knowledge
- Sentinels suddenly activate after centuries, suggesting some great threat approaches

- ### Scaling
- **Lower CR:** Reduce hit points and remove legendary actions for CR 5-6 version
- **Higher CR:** Add spell-like abilities or multiple sentinel coordination for CR 10-12 encounters
