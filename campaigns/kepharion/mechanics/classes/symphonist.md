- # Symphonist

- **Hit Die:** d10 per symphonist level
- **Primary Abilities:** Charisma and Intelligence
- **Saving Throw Proficiencies:** Charisma, Intelligence
- **Spellcasting:** Full caster using Charisma
- **[[Xenocortex]] Specialization:** Multi-Variable Coordination and Leadership Networks

- Symphonists are the coordinators, integrators, and visionary leaders who weave diverse talents into harmonious achievements. They excel at high-level planning, governance, and multi-tasking through [[xenocortex]]-enhanced parallel processing and group coordination abilities.

- ## Class Features

- ### 1st Level

- #### Hit Points
- **Hit Points at 1st Level:** 10 + Constitution modifier
- **Hit Points at Higher Levels:** 1d10 (or 6) + Constitution modifier per symphonist level after 1st

- #### Proficiencies
- **Armor:** All armor, shields
- **Weapons:** Simple weapons, martial weapons
- **Tools:** One type of artisan's tools, one gaming set
- **Saving Throws:** Charisma, Intelligence

- #### Skills
			- Choose 4 from: Athletics, Deception, History, Insight, Intimidation, Investigation, Perception, Persuasion

- #### Coordination [[Xenocortex]]
			- Your [[xenocortex]] is specialized for multi-variable analysis and group coordination:
- **[[Xenocortex]] Rating increases by 1** at 1st level and again at 3rd, 6th, 10th, 14th, and 18th levels
- **Parallel Processing:** Can maintain concentration on two spells simultaneously (higher level spell determines save DC)
- **System Analysis:** Advantage on Intelligence checks to understand complex systems or organizations

- #### Leadership Presence
			- Your commanding presence inspires and coordinates others:
- **Command Voice:** Allies within 30 feet add your Charisma modifier to initiative rolls
- **Tactical Coordination:** As a bonus action, can grant one ally within 60 feet advantage on their next attack roll
- **Unity Field:** Allies within 10 feet gain +1 to AC while you're conscious

- #### Spellcasting (Full Caster)
			- You can cast symphonist spells using Charisma as your spellcasting ability:
- **Spell Slots:** Use full caster progression (like Sorcerer)
- **Spells Known:** Charisma modifier + symphonist level
- **Ritual Casting:** Can cast spells with ritual tag as rituals

- ### 2nd Level

- #### Harmonic Leadership
			- You can synchronize group actions:
- **Group Coordination:** Once per turn, can allow an ally to take a reaction when you take an action
- **Efficiency Protocols:** During short rests, allies regain additional hit points equal to your Charisma modifier
- **[[Xenocortex]] Network:** Can form temporary mental links with willing allies for tactical communication

- ### 3rd Level

- #### Symphonist Conducting Style
			- Choose your leadership specialization:

- ##### The Grand Conductor
- **Strategic Overview:** Can see the battlefield from multiple perspectives simultaneously
- **Master Tactician:** Allies gain bonus to damage equal to your Intelligence modifier when following your plans
- **Battle Rhythm:** Can change initiative order of willing allies once per combat

- ##### The Diplomat
- **Social Networks:** Double proficiency bonus on Charisma checks in political or social situations
- **Harmonic Negotiation:** Can calm hostile creatures through [[xenocortex]]-enhanced persuasion
- **Alliance Building:** Can form temporary alliances even with traditionally hostile creatures

- ##### The Architect
- **Systems Design:** Can analyze and improve organizational or magical systems
- **Resource Optimization:** Spells cast through careful planning cost one less spell slot (minimum 1st level)
- **Infrastructure Command:** Can temporarily enhance buildings, vehicles, or constructs

- ### 4th Level

- #### Ability Score Improvement
			- You can increase one ability score by 2, or two ability scores by 1. You can also choose a feat.

- #### Enhanced Coordination
			- Your leadership abilities expand:
- **Multi-Tasking Master:** Can maintain concentration on three different effects (spells, abilities, etc.)
- **Command Efficiency:** Can use your tactical coordination as a free action
- **Network Expansion:** Your unity field range increases to 15 feet

- ### 5th Level

- #### Symphonic Casting
			- Your spellcasting becomes a coordinated effort:
- **Harmonic Amplification:** When allies are within your unity field, spell save DCs increase by 1
- **Distributed Casting:** Can have allies provide verbal or somatic components for your spells
- **Resonant Magic:** Spells that target allies automatically succeed on beneficial effects

- ### 6th Level

- #### Conducting Style Feature
			- Your chosen style grants advanced abilities:

- ##### The Grand Conductor
- **Perfect Strategy:** Once per long rest, can give your entire party advantage on all rolls for one round
- **Command Presence:** Can issue complex battle commands that allies execute flawlessly
- **Tactical Prescience:** Can predict enemy actions through [[xenocortex]] pattern analysis

- ##### The Diplomat
- **Master Negotiator:** Can prevent combat through negotiation even in extreme circumstances
- **Cultural Synthesis:** Can understand and adapt to any cultural context rapidly
- **Peaceful Resolution:** Can cast *calm emotions* and *zone of truth* at will

- ##### The Architect  
- **System Integration:** Can permanently improve magical or technological systems
- **Resource Multiplication:** Can make limited resources stretch further through efficient coordination
- **Infrastructure Mastery:** Can control and modify large-scale constructions or vehicles

- ### Higher Level Features

- #### 10th Level - Master Coordination
- **[[Xenocortex]] Symphony:** Can link xenocortices of multiple Vaarn for enhanced group processing
- **Leadership Aura:** Your unity field affects all allies who can see or hear you

- #### 14th Level - Systemic Mastery
- **Organizational Override:** Can take temporary control of hostile organizations or systems
- **Harmonic Domination:** Can coordinate unwilling creatures through overwhelming [[xenocortex]] presence

- #### 18th Level - Grand Symphony
- **Reality Coordination:** Can coordinate multiple planes or dimensions simultaneously
- **Universal Harmony:** Can bring temporary peace and cooperation to any conflict

- ## AI Decision Making Preferences

- ### Combat Leadership
- **Primary Actions:** ["coordinate_allies", "cast_support_spell", "tactical_positioning"]
- **Target Priority:** ["optimize_group_performance", "eliminate_key_threats", "control_battlefield"]
- **Positioning:** Central location with sight lines to all allies
- **Risk Tolerance:** Moderate (calculated risks for strategic advantage)

- ### Strategic Decision Making
		- ```ruby
		- def symphonist_strategy_assessment(battlefield_state)
		- threat_analysis = assess_all_threats(battlefield_state)
		- ally_capabilities = analyze_ally_potential(battlefield_state[:allies])
		- resource_efficiency = calculate_optimal_resource_use(battlefield_state)
  
- strategy = {
- threat_priority: threat_analysis[:highest_threat],
- coordination_plan: optimize_ally_actions(ally_capabilities),
- resource_allocation: resource_efficiency[:optimal_distribution]
- }
  
- return execute_coordinated_response(strategy)
- end

- def leadership_style_adaptation(group_composition)
- if group_composition[:combat_heavy]
- return :grand_conductor_tactics
- elsif group_composition[:social_focused]
- return :diplomatic_harmony
- else
- return :architectural_coordination
- end
- end
- ```

- ## AI Personality Integration

- **Risk Averse:** Uses careful planning and coordination to minimize risks
- **Risk Seeking:** Takes calculated strategic risks for maximum group benefit
- **Group Focused:** Extremely strong emphasis on group coordination and success
- **Self Focused:** Balances personal power with responsibility to coordinate others

- **Armor:** Medium to heavy armor with command and communication enhancements
- **Tools:** Command instruments, [[xenocortex]] network interfaces, strategic planning tools
- **Special:** Group coordination devices, leadership regalia, tactical communication systems
