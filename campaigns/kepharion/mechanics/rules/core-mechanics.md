# Kepharion Core Mechanics

## Currency System
**Energy Credits** - Universal currency for technology, travel, and energy-based transactions.

## Magic vs Technology
- **Magic Exists** - Traditional magical abilities are present
- **Technomancy** - Technology that appears magical (proton pack-style weapons, nanite healing)
- **Dual Nature** - Both systems coexist, sometimes indistinguishably

## Token-Based Casting System
Instead of traditional spell slots, characters use resource tokens:
- **Essence Tokens** - For casting magical spells
- **Energy Tokens** - For operating advanced technology
- **Pattern Tokens** - For accessing xenocortex-stored abilities

## Multiverse Travel
- **Toll System** - Traversing between universes always has negative costs
- **Sanity Drain** - Multiverse travel reduces sanity score
- **Reality Strain** - Extended multiverse exposure causes mental fatigue

## Experience System
**Milestone Advancement** - Characters advance through story achievements rather than traditional XP.

## FTL Travel Methods
- **Wormhole Navigation** - Requires specialized pilot training
- **Magical Portals** - Arcane methods of space folding
- **Folding Space Drive** - Technology-based space compression
- **Pilot Requirements** - All FTL methods need trained helmsman/pilot
- **Event Horizon Risk** - Failed FTL attempts can trap vessels in dimensional anomalies

## Sanity System
**Base Sanity Score**: Constitution modifier + Intelligence modifier + Wisdom modifier
- **Sanity Pool** - Total mental resilience points
- **Sanity Loss** - Multiverse travel, cosmic horror, pattern conflicts
- **Recovery** - Rest, meditation, xenocortex pattern harmonization

## Xenocortex Integration
- **Pattern Encoding** - Store successful behaviors during rest cycles
- **Cognitive Specialization** - Choose Pattern-Breaker, Pattern-Holder, or Harmony Walker path
- **Memory Permanence** - Encoded patterns become permanent but inflexible abilities

## Character Creation Modifications
- **Xenocortex Rating** - New ability score (1-20)
- **Pattern Capacity** - Based on xenocortex rating and Constitution
- **Cultural Archetype** - Replaces traditional backgrounds with cognitive philosophy
- **Starting Patterns** - Begin with encoded behaviors from background

## Rest and Recovery
- **Short Rest** - 1 hour, restore some tokens
- **Long Rest** - 8 hours, full recovery + pattern encoding opportunity
- **Deep Rest** - 24+ hours, encode complex patterns or recover from sanity loss
